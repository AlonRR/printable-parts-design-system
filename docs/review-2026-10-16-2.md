# Review of v0.5.0, 16 Oct 2026 (second round)

Five review agents read `main` at `fb4f052` (tag `v0.5.0`), each from one angle: accessibility
(WCAG 2.2 AA), usability on phones and desktop, visual design and typography, the adopter's path and
browser support, and internal consistency with the checks' blind spots. They were told the
decisions D25 to D37 and the items knowingly left, and reported only what is new. This file merges
their reports. Marks: **verified** means checked against the files or a render by a reviewer;
**verified (session)** means the session that merged them checked it again; **relayed** means one
reviewer's measurement, not repeated. The decisions it raises are A38 and D39 to D42 in `STATUS.md`.

Two reviewers wrote screenshots into the repository's root by mistake and moved them out; the
working tree was clean afterwards (verified (session): `git status`).

## What holds, and should not change

- Tab order is logical (23 stops on a desktop, 18 on a phone), every stop shows the 2px ring, and
  on a phone `scroll-padding` keeps a focused field clear of the pinned part (verified).
- More… and View… report their expanded state, take focus in order, close on Escape with focus back
  on their button, and stay open through repeated Zoom in taps (verified).
- The drawing is an image named "The wall in 3D", described by the plan line; the view choice with
  nothing checked is a valid radio group (verified).
- Targets: the smallest is 36px with a mouse and 44px on touch; text spacing (1.4.12) clips nothing
  new; every rendered text run, 980 of them with hover and focus forced, is at least 4.5:1
  (verified).
- Every class and token name the docs mention exists; `FONTS.md`'s subset ranges match both fonts
  exactly (verified). The full migration, done completely, renders right in Chromium, WebKit and
  Firefox, apart from item 1 below (verified).
- On a portrait phone the view gets its 45 %, the plan bar and the primary button stay reachable,
  nothing scrolls sideways from 320 to 932px, and long Choice labels wrap cleanly (verified).

## Findings, by theme

### 1. Two bugs in released versions (high)

- **In WebKit the View… menu covers the whole screen** (v0.5.0). On a 375 × 667 phone it opens
  326 × 659 from 8px below the top, over the button that opened it (verified (session): WebKit
  26.0). A grid popover stretches to the screen's height in WebKit
  ([bug 270334](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=270334); still in Safari 26.5, relayed);
  every iOS browser is WebKit. A flex popover anchors correctly (verified). Fix: `display:flex`
  with wrapping, the choice on its own line.
- **The wide-screen header rules never apply** (v0.4.0 and v0.5.0). The phone rules come after the
  `@media (min-width:56rem)` block at the same specificity, so on a desktop the tool name is 15px
  (`legend`), not 17.6px (`step`), the header buttons get 8px padding, not 12px, and the spacer is
  hidden (verified (session): computed styles at 960px). This came in with `1e164a3` (D28) and was
  not caught: the checks test layout, not which rule wins. Fix: the phone rules before the block,
  and a build check that a selector set inside `@media` does not appear again unconditionally
  later (it flags exactly these three rules today, relayed).

### 2. Screens the checks never render (high)

`(min-aspect-ratio:4/3)` puts the sidebar beside the stage on any screen wider than it is tall,
however narrow. The checked "zoomed" screen is 320 × 256 (5:4), which stacks, so the checks never
see it.

- **400 % zoom on a 16:9 screen** (1280 × 720 → 320 × 180): the 3D view is 16px tall, its controls
  are drawn outside it and the View… menu opens off the screen (verified (session): the check's own
  layout code reports 7 stage failures). 300 % on 16:9 and 16:10 behave alike (relayed).
- **200 % zoom on 1280 × 720** (640 × 360): the view is 104px, under D25's 40 % (verified
  (session)).
- **Small landscape phones**: 740 × 360 gives the view 111px (31 %), 667 × 375 gives 126px (34 %);
  the plan bar wraps to 160px beside the sidebar (verified (session)).

Decision **D41**. Either way the four screens join the checked ones.

### 3. The plan sentence on a phone (high)

Beside a primary button that never wraps, the sentence is 70px wide at 375px (about eight
characters a line: "4 pieces · 1 hos…") and 15px at 320px ("4 p…") (verified (session)). The
plan, the file name, build errors and the first-visit line are then only in the page source, for
screen readers; the line is the 3D view's text equivalent (D30). D25 chose one row; nothing set a
floor for the sentence. Decision **D39**.

### 4. What the checks let through (high)

52 realistic one-line mistakes were made in a copy and run through the gate: **7 caught, 3 caught
by a side effect, 42 not caught** (relayed; two re-run by the session and confirmed not caught:
the button focus ring deleted, and the phone view unpinned).

- **States are never rendered.** Deleting the pressed state of a tile, piece, Sides button or the
  current step, the invalid field, either disabled state, or any of five focus rings passes;
  `ring: 0px` passes too. Fix: force `:hover` and `:focus-visible` through CDP on every control and
  assert the ring, and tint, ring and bold on every pressed, checked or current control.
- **More… is never opened**: its desktop row hidden (Save and Load unreachable), its phone menu off
  the screen, or a `popovertarget` typo all pass. Fix: test it as View… is tested.
- **Pinning is unchecked**: the view or the plan bar unpinned on a phone passes. Fix: scroll and
  assert where both sit.
- **Touch size is relative to a token**: `tap: 30px` passes; tiles are in no size list; a Choice
  face 46px tall passes as "at least". Fix: 44px absolute on touch, tiles listed, one-line faces
  exact.
- **Contrast is checked on listed token pairs**, not on what is drawn: a note in `border-strong`,
  an error in `ink-muted`, a token re-set inside `bundle.css`, `color-mix()` or a system colour all
  pass. Fix: a rendered text-contrast check, and only `--control-h` may be re-set, only for touch.
- **The typed-value guard has holes**: `@container`, a typed `opacity`, `font-size:smaller`,
  `line-height:2` and a gradient pass the build.
- **`check-previews.py` has no controls**, though `DESIGN.md` and `README.md` say every check has
  them in `tests/`; a broken font query passes silently (a `try`/`except`), a one-letter selector
  typo drops the Sides labels from the centring check, and the `primary` count counts pages, so it
  can never be zero. Fix: `tests/test-check-previews.sh` with one control per check, and a count per
  selector.
- **Coverage hides behind totals**: a deleted component folder, a dropped preview variant, a
  removed `role` or `aria-pressed`, or a README naming a removed class all pass; 22 of the 63
  classes in `bundle.css` are named in no doc.

### 5. Browsers (medium)

No browser target is stated anywhere, and every check runs only in Chromium; that is how item 1
got through (verified: grep, `check.yml`, Skådis Air's Playwright config). Running
`check-previews.py` in Playwright's WebKit and Firefox works here (verified). Support, from MDN's
data (relayed): popover 114 / 17 / 125 (Chrome / Safari / Firefox), `position-area` 129 / 26 / 147,
`:has()` 105 / 15.4 / 121, `svh` 108 / 15.4 / 101. Without anchoring, the View… menu opens
mid-screen over the primary button (verified). Safari has no `ascent-override`, so labels sit
1.4px high there against 0.4px in Chromium (relayed). The Sides box's `content: "" / ""` needs
Safari 17.4 and Firefox 128; older engines draw no box (inferred). Decision **D40**.

### 6. The adopter's path (medium)

- Skådis Air copies **v0.2.0**, not v0.3.0 as this repository has said (verified: its
  `web/public/design-system/README.md`, local `a9c9a93` and `origin/main` `b55c32d`), and v0.3.0's
  entry has no "Moving to it": five class renames (`preview-message`, `plan-line`, `plan-actions`,
  `hint-fine`, `hint-touch` to `ws-` names), `class="ws-page"` and `ws-notice--warning` are written
  nowhere as steps. Skipped, the hints run together, the plan sentence runs to six lines and
  "Download STL" is clipped (verified, WebKit, Firefox and Chromium).
- "Drop the local rules" names none. Kept, `.ws-stage__view{position:relative}` unpins the phone
  view, `.ws-header__name{margin:0}` pulls More… beside the name, and
  `.ws-notice:empty{display:none}` undoes the live-region change (verified).
- Following the v0.4.0 note to drop `.ws-notice strong{color:var(--warning)}` turns Skådis Air's
  warnings grey, as its warnings are plain `.ws-notice` (verified).
- The header step assumes Undo and Redo and a full More…; Skådis Air has one action and fits at
  375px without More… (verified).

### 7. Accessibility details (medium)

- **Forced colours**: the chosen radio's dot disappears (its background and inset shadow are
  dropped), and a done step looks like a step not reached (verified, emulated); the CSS comment
  promising "a filled circle" is wrong. The build allows no `forced-colors` query.
- **A focused, selected tile or piece differs from a focused, unselected one by colour alone**
  (1.13:1 light): the focus offset moves the selection ring, and they keep a grey border inside it
  where Choice, Sides and the rail turn theirs `action` (verified).
- **The ring inside the 3D view** is 1.02:1 against `model-panel` in dark (verified by calculation);
  no pair in `check-contrast.py` covers it.
- **A popover left open across the breakpoint** (a tablet turned) floats over the header with its
  button hidden (verified); only the tool's script can close it on the change.
- Smaller: no `main` landmark (the form is in `aside`); Gallery and PieceMap have no group name; the
  Dialog's `menu` is a list without items; no hint names `+` and `−`.

### 8. The View… menu in use (medium)

Open, it covers 33 % of the view at 375 × 667 and 44 % at 844 × 390, and stays open over the part
after Front, Back or Top is picked (verified). The view's controls also cover the part's corner on
a phone, as the part is framed to the whole view (verified). Decision **D42**.

### 9. Visual consistency (medium to low)

- The Dialog runs edge to edge on a phone: its `max-width` is the content box (verified).
- The modal backdrop is the browser's own; the dialog against the dimmed page is 1.25:1 light and
  1.03:1 dark (verified).
- The phone header still clips the top and bottom of a focus ring (`overflow-x:auto`) (verified).
- Icons sit 0.7 to 1.75px below the capitals: every Codicon is centred at 600 units against a cap
  centre of 688 (verified with fontTools); the centring check measures labels, not icons.
- Hover: a disabled Button or Choice takes the hover tint; tiles, pieces, steps and summaries have
  no hover at all (verified).
- Vertical rhythm: the gap from a legend to the first item is 16, 20 or 24px, and from a group to
  its note 0, 4 or 8px (verified).
- Two ellipsis forms on one screen ("pieces..." and "More…"); the preview's header still says
  v0.3.0; the More… items are centred so their icons do not line up; in dark `surface-sunken` is
  lighter than `surface-raised` (verified).

### 10. Docs against code (low)

Of about 180 statements checked, 17 disagree (relayed): the header's style (item 1); the claimed
controls (item 4); "a colour in any notation"; token usage texts that describe the white ring,
an edge toggle, a 92 % dialog and the pre-D26 fill; marks said to sit `icon-gap` that sit
`icon-gap` plus `ring`; the unused `code` style; "no shadows" against the radio's inset shadow;
two component READMEs; `check-previews.py` typing `56rem` and `30rem` instead of reading tokens.

### 11. Fonts (low)

The two fonts are 331 KB, about 140 KB of each being icons the tools do not use, shipped twice;
text alone is 27 and 29 KB (relayed). Splitting by `unicode-range` and preloading the regular text
face would cut the first paint's download. `FONTS.md`'s "punctuation, arrows" is nine marks and
four arrows; ≤ ≥ ≈ ⌀ and Hebrew are absent (relayed).
