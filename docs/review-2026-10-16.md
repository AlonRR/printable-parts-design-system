# Review of the design principles, 16 Oct 2026

Five review agents read `main` at `3cc4928`, the rendered previews and Skådis Air's page
(`AlonRR/skadis-air`), each from one angle: accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA), usability, visual design
and typography, architecture and upkeep, and internal consistency (about 140 claims checked, 36 not
matching). This file merges their reports. Marks: **verified** means checked against the files or a
render (by a reviewer, and the key ones again by the session that merged them); **relayed** means
one reviewer's measurement, not repeated. The decisions it raises are D25 to D33 in `STATUS.md`.

## What holds, and should not change

- `tokens.json` as the one source, `tokens.css` generated and held by CI; the byte-exact copy from a
  tag; vertical centring set in the font; `uv --locked`.
- The colours: every one of the 52 checked pairs passes in both themes, and the dark theme is
  designed, not inverted. Every selection state has a mark besides colour, and both survive forced
  colours (relayed).
- One typeface; Codicons beside a label and hidden from assistive tech, enforced by a check.
- Choice buttons that are native radios underneath; inline Notices, never toasts; units in every
  label; "starting values" honesty; no motion.
- The part always fits the stage (D15), with its browser test in Skådis Air.

## Findings, by theme

### 1. On a phone the stage is mostly bars, and it hides what has focus (high)

DESIGN gives the *stage* 45 % of the height, but the plan bar and status bar sit inside it. At
375 × 667 the 3D view gets about 55 to 71 px of the stage's 300; at 320 × 256 (a phone at 200 %
text) and 640 × 400 (1280 × 800 at 200 % zoom) it gets 0 px; a landscape phone stacks and gets
about 42 px (relayed, measured with Playwright). The stage is sticky with no `scroll-padding`, so
on a short screen a focused control scrolls under it (WCAG 2.4.11), and a phone keyboard leaves
the field about 60 px (inferred). The screenshots hide all of this: since the D18 screenshot fix,
the phone record is drawn in a window grown to the page (1087 px tall), where the stage is 489 px
(verified by construction). Decision **D25**.

### 2. The reference markup and the prose disagree (high)

All verified:

- The Choice and Sides READMEs and a bundle.css comment say the chosen Choice "starts with a tick";
  the CSS draws an inverted radio, as DESIGN and the check require.
- The Workbench README says the rail runs 1 to 6; DESIGN and the preview say 5.
- The Workbench preview, the reference, breaks DESIGN's own rules: a `<select>` for a short choice,
  two primary buttons at once, number fields without `inputmode` (so the width cap misses them), a
  theme toggle labelled "Theme" without `aria-pressed`, no Reset, a `span` for the name.
- The Notice uses `radius-md`; DESIGN and the token say `radius-sm`. DESIGN names `title` for the
  tool name; the CSS uses `step`. The status bar is not uppercase, though DESIGN says `label-caps`
  is, because the generated shorthand cannot carry case and tracking (`.08em` is typed again).
- The Dialog preview is a `div role="dialog"` with inline spacing that overrides bundle.css.
- Token `usage` texts name uses the CSS does not make (`border` for the stage hairline, which is
  `divider`; `action-tint` for the active step; `radius-md` for a "stage frame").
- `docs/adoption-skadis-air.md` still says to copy `v0.2.0`, and `web/design-system/` instead of
  `web/public/design-system/`. DESIGN says Skådis Air's browser test has 8 tests; it defines 8
  and runs 12 (four run once per device). The old repository name is left in CLAUDE.md and in
  `tokens.json`'s unread `meta`.

Fix: one pass over the prose and previews (no design change), plus checks so they cannot drift
again: no `style=` in previews other than the covers, at most one primary button per view, number
fields carry `inputmode`, every check prints its count and fails at zero.

### 3. "No value is typed twice" is enforced for hex colours only (high)

`build-tokens.py` fails on `#hex`, `@font-face` and one breakpoint string. A scratch copy of
bundle.css with `rgb()`, `hsl()`, a named colour, a second `@media` width, another `font-family`,
an undefined `var(--acton)` and a raw `13px` passed (relayed). Values typed today (verified):
`font-weight:600` six times and `bold` once, `.08em`, `--sides-w`/`--sides-h` defined in
bundle.css, `45vh`, `1px` 16 times. The type tokens ask for weights 500, 600 and 750, but only
400 and 700 ship, so 600 and 750 draw at 700 and 500 at 400 (verified). Fix: the build fails on
an undefined `var()`, a colour function or name, any other `@media` width, any `font-family` but
`--font-ui`, lengths outside an allowlist and a weight without a `@font-face`; each kept as a
control in a test; tokens for weight, tracking, border width and the phone stage.

### 4. The 3D stage needs a pointer (high)

Turning takes a drag, zooming a pinch or the wheel: no keyboard path, no single-pointer
alternative (WCAG 2.1.1, 2.5.7), and the canvas has no accessible name (verified in Skådis Air's
`preview.ts`). The stage message has no backing, so on the model it drops to 2.37:1 (light) and
1.00:1 (dark) (relayed, computed). The `model-*` colours are the same in both themes, so on the
dark stage `model-tpu` is 1.29:1 and `model-outline` 1.90:1 (relayed, computed; the 3D lighting
changes the real pixels). Decision **D30**; the backing and the material pairs need none.

### 5. Live regions and form wiring (medium)

The empty-region rule D18 added (`.ws-notice:empty`, `.ws-stage__message:empty` with
`display:none`) takes the region out of the accessibility tree, so screen readers may stay silent
when it fills (verified that it leaves the tree; the silence is inferred): collapse it with zero
padding, border and margin instead. Field errors and notes are not linked to their inputs
(`aria-describedby`); the plan line has no `aria-live`; warnings never reach the plan line, which
on a phone is the only line always in view. Skådis Air empties the stage on an invalid value
instead of keeping the last good part, and its plan line points at "the settings marked above",
which on a desktop are to the right (relayed).

### 6. How selection looks (medium)

A chosen Choice and a pressed Sides button are the same solid `action` fill as the primary button
("STL" and "Download" look alike in the Dialog), while Gallery and PieceMap show selection as a
tint and a ring. Sides has no mark when not pressed, so pressing adds a 16 px tick and shifts the
grid (relayed). The `choice` amber border reads as `warning` (ΔE 9, 1.13:1 in dark, relayed) and
can never apply, as every Choice has a default (verified). Decisions **D26** and **D27**.

### 7. The font (medium)

Fira Code's ligatures rewrite data: the file name `1280x420mm` renders `1280×420mm` (verified:
the source has `x`, the render `×`). In the subset every icon has a one-cell advance but several
draw past it, so the icon-to-label gap varies from about 2.3 to 6.9 px (relayed, measured with
fontTools); building the subset from the proportional-icon variant would fix it at the source.
Decision **D29** for the ligatures.

### 8. Smaller items

- The header on a phone shows Undo and Redo; Save, Load and the theme scroll sideways with no cue,
  and the touch-size buttons clip their focus ring. Decision **D28**.
- The step rail is required, though Skådis Air chose one form (its D13), and its buttons are bare
  numbers to a screen reader. Decision **D31**.
- No changelog; three version numbers that disagree (pyproject 0.1.0, `tokens.json` 1, tag
  v0.3.0); `tokens.css` emits classes without the `ws-` prefix (`.note`, `.code`, `.section` and
  five more) that Skådis Air already relies on. Decision **D32**.
- "Imperial optional" in DESIGN against metric-only. Decision **D33**.
- Numeric fields: iOS's decimal keypad has no minus, and Skådis Air takes −0.5 mm; no stepper for
  stepped sizes; "," as a decimal mark is not accepted (relayed).
- No rule for links (colour set inline in a preview, missing from the focus-ring list); the
  PieceMap gap lets the selected outline touch its neighbours; the Cover's dark piece turns white in
  dark; the status bar breaks mid-phrase (it needs `min-height` and wrapping between items).
- "Live" has no performance rule: a slow build blocks typing (inferred).
- The project layers (Sides, PieceMap, model colours, covers) live in the base files and are named
  in the checks; `check-contrast.py`'s pair list is hand-kept, so 5 of 21 colours are in no pair.
- CI runs every job twice on a pull request and installs Chromium uncached.
