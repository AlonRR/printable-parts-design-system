The slim top bar of the tool: name, project actions, theme.

Height `header-h` (48px). Left: the tool name (`h1.ws-header__name`, in the `step` style) and, small beside it, the version (`.ws-header__version`), then a `span.ws-header__spacer`, which pushes the buttons to the right on a wide screen. Right, in this order: undo, redo, reset (quiet buttons), Save project, Load project (they act on the whole project), language if any, the "Dark theme" toggle (`aria-pressed`, pressed while the page is dark). Everything after redo goes in `div.ws-header__actions` with `popover`, opened by a `button.ws-header__more` "More…" (`popovertarget`): inline from `breakpoint-header` (64rem) up, a menu under the header below it (D28). The version goes in twice: beside the name, and again last in `.ws-header__actions`, after the theme toggle, `<span class="ws-header__version">v1.2.0</span>`. Below `breakpoint-header` the stylesheet hides the one in the row and the More… menu shows the other: in the row it left a phone 10 of the name's 120px at 320px wide and 65 at 375 (D51). From `breakpoint-header` up the row's shows and the menu's is hidden. The hidden copy is `display:none`, so a screen reader hears one version, beside the name on a wide screen and in the open menu on a phone, with the rest of the actions; `check-previews.py` fails if the row holds a version below `breakpoint-header`, or if more or fewer than one shows. `breakpoint-header` is wider than the layout's breakpoint (56rem): from 56rem the whole row left the name as little as 20px, so on a window 56 to 64rem wide the sidebar sits beside the stage while the header keeps its phone row (D49). The browser gives the menu its expanded state, Esc and light dismiss; no script is needed. A tool whose header holds no more than fits a phone's row (Skådis Air's holds the theme toggle alone) leaves out More… and the popover, and its buttons and its one version stay in the row on every screen. Below `breakpoint-header` the name takes the legend size. When the row is tight, on a narrow phone, the name is cut short (its full text stays in the page): the buttons never shrink, scroll or wrap, and nothing in the header clips a focus ring. From the layout's breakpoint up the name is whole, and `check-previews.py` fails if it is cut (D49). Everything here acts on the whole project; step controls never go here. Each button has a Nerd Font icon (undo eae2, redo ebb0, reset eb37, save eb4b, load eaf7, theme eac6) before its word; the icon is `aria-hidden` and the word is always there.
