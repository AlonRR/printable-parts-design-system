Action button, `button.ws-btn`. `.ws-btn--primary` fills with `action`; the default is outlined; `.ws-btn--quiet` has neither border nor fill, for the header's undo, redo and reset.

Use at most one primary button in view, for the step that finishes the task (Download the pieces). Label it with a verb and, when it opens a dialog, end the label with an ellipsis. In the plan bar on a phone it shows a short label, "Download...", the rest of its label in a `.ws-plan__long` span hidden from view only, so its name stays whole and the ellipsis stays on screen (D39; see `Workbench`). A disabled button explains why in a note next to it. A button that must keep its focus while it cannot be used, a `Stepper`'s at a limit, takes `aria-disabled="true"` instead of `disabled`: it is drawn as a disabled one and takes no hover, keeps its place in the tab order, and the tool's script ignores a press on it; disabled, the focused button would lose its focus (Firefox drops it to the page, D56). Buttons are `type="button"` unless they submit a form. Put a row of buttons in `.ws-actions`; the plan bar (`.ws-plan__actions`) and a dialog's `menu` have their own.
