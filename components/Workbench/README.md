The page layout of the tool: header, the 3D stage with a status bar, and a sidebar with a numbered step rail.

Put `class="ws-page"` on `<body>`: it drops the browser's margin and sets the page's background, text colour and font, form controls included. Build it as four parts. (1) `.ws-header`: tool name (`h1.ws-header__name`) at left, then undo and redo, then a `.ws-header__more` "More…" button and the `.ws-header__actions` popover it opens (`popovertarget`) holding reset, Save project, Load project and the theme toggle. Above the breakpoint the popover sits inline and More… is hidden; below it, the popover opens under the header (D28). (2) `.ws-stage`: the 3D view fills it, with a `.ws-status` bar under it (file or part info, units, and the hints) and, above that, a `.ws-plan` bar: the plan's sentence in `.ws-plan__line` (`aria-live="polite"`, so a change is announced), then `.ws-plan__actions` with the primary button. Side by side, the sentence takes the room the buttons leave, down to `--plan-line-basis`; narrower, the buttons move under it, and a long file name breaks rather than widening the page. On a phone the bar is one row, the sentence at most two lines. The view's own controls ("Reset view") go in `.ws-stage__tools`, top right over the view. The hints are two spans, `.ws-hint-fine` for a mouse and `.ws-hint-touch` for a touch screen, each naming the gestures the tool supports ("Left drag: orbit · Scroll: zoom", "Drag: turn · Pinch: zoom"); the stylesheet shows the one that fits the screen. (3) `.ws-side`: a `.ws-rail` with one numbered button per step (`aria-current="step"` on the active one) and `.ws-controls` with the step title, its fieldsets, and Back/Next. (4) Planned, not yet a component: an edge toggle on the sidebar's border that collapses it so the stage gets the full width.

The tool's 3D view is a `canvas` placed directly in `.ws-stage__view`: the stylesheet makes it fill the view and never size it, so a resize cannot feed back into itself, and turns off the browser's own touch gestures on it (`touch-action:none`) so drag and pinch reach the view. A `p.ws-stage__message` (`aria-live="polite"`) in the view says what is happening while the part loads or when it cannot be drawn, on a `surface-raised` backing so it reads over the model; empty, it takes no room but stays in the page, so it is announced when it fills.

The part in the stage always fits it (DESIGN.md): framed to `--stage-fit` when the part or its size changes, pulled back only as far as needed when the stage resizes (keeping the user's angle), zoom stopping at `--stage-min-fill`, no panning off the stage, and "Reset view" back to the first view.

The stage is always mounted and always visible. On a phone the stage lays its parts out in the page (`display:contents`): the view, `--stage-phone-h` tall, and the plan bar are pinned, the status bar scrolls away with the form, and the page's `scroll-padding` keeps a focused field below the pinned part; under `--breakpoint-short` nothing is pinned. On a screen `--breakpoint-wide` or wider, or at least `--landscape-ratio` wide, the stage takes the full height beside the sidebar (`panel-width`, at most half the screen) (D25). Never put the result under the inputs. On first visit show a short welcome dialog (a quick start in 4 numbered steps and a line that everything runs in the browser); keep it dismissible and show it once.
