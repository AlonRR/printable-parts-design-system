The page layout of the tool: header, the 3D stage with a status bar, and a sidebar with a numbered step rail.

Put `class="ws-page"` on `<body>`: it drops the browser's margin and sets the page's background, text colour and font, form controls included. Build it as four parts. (1) `.ws-header`: tool name (`h1.ws-header__name`) at left, then undo, redo and reset, Save project and Load project, and a theme toggle at right. (2) `.ws-stage`: the 3D view fills it, with a `.ws-status` bar under it (file or part info, units, and the hints) and, above that, a `.ws-plan` bar: the plan's sentence in `.ws-plan__line`, then `.ws-plan__actions` with "Reset view" and the primary button. The sentence takes the room the buttons leave, down to `--plan-line-basis`; narrower, the buttons move under it, and a long file name in it breaks rather than widening the page. The hints are two spans, `.ws-hint-fine` for a mouse and `.ws-hint-touch` for a touch screen, each naming the gestures the tool supports ("Left drag: orbit · Scroll: zoom", "Drag: turn · Pinch: zoom"); the stylesheet shows the one that fits the screen. (3) `.ws-side`: a `.ws-rail` with one numbered button per step (`aria-current="step"` on the active one) and `.ws-controls` with the step title, its fieldsets, and Back/Next. (4) Planned, not yet a component: an edge toggle on the sidebar's border that collapses it so the stage gets the full width.

The tool's 3D view is a `canvas` placed directly in `.ws-stage__view`: the stylesheet makes it fill the view and never size it, so a resize cannot feed back into itself, and turns off the browser's own touch gestures on it (`touch-action:none`) so drag and pinch reach the view. A `p.ws-stage__message` in the view says what is happening while the part loads or when it cannot be drawn; empty, it takes no room.

The part in the stage always fits it (DESIGN.md): framed to `--stage-fit` when the part or its size changes, pulled back only as far as needed when the stage resizes (keeping the user's angle), zoom stopping at `--stage-min-fill`, no panning off the stage, and "Reset view" back to the first view.

The stage is always mounted and always visible: 45% of the screen height on phones, the full height beside the 380px sidebar on screens 56rem and wider. Never put the result under the inputs. On first visit show a short welcome dialog (a quick start in 4 numbered steps and a line that everything runs in the browser); keep it dismissible and show it once.
