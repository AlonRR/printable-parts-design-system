The page layout of the tool: header, the 3D stage with a status bar, and a sidebar, with a step rail once the form outgrows a screen.

Put `class="ws-page"` on `<body>`: it drops the browser's margin and sets the page's background, text colour and font, form controls included. Build it as four parts in a `.ws-workbench`. (1) `.ws-header`: tool name (`h1.ws-header__name`) at left, then undo and redo, then a `.ws-header__more` "More…" button and the `.ws-header__actions` popover it opens (`popovertarget`) holding reset, Save project, Load project and the theme toggle. From `--breakpoint-header` up the popover sits inline and More… is hidden; below it, the popover opens under the header (D28). That is wider than `--breakpoint-wide`, so the tool's name stays whole beside the sidebar (D49; see `Header`). (2) `.ws-stage`: the 3D view fills it, with a `.ws-status` bar under it (file or part info, units, and the hints) and, above that, a `.ws-plan` bar: the plan's sentence in `.ws-plan__line` (`aria-live="polite"`, so a change is announced), then `.ws-plan__actions` with the primary button. Side by side, the sentence takes the room the buttons leave, down to `--plan-line-basis`; narrower, the buttons move under it, and a long file name breaks rather than widening the page. On a phone the bar is one row, the sentence at most two lines. The view's own controls go over it, in `.ws-stage__tools` at the top right: "Reset view", then the "View…" button and its menu holding "Zoom in", "Zoom out" and the Front · Back · Top choice (D30, D36; below). The hints are two spans, `.ws-hint-fine` for a mouse and `.ws-hint-touch` for a touch screen, each naming the gestures the tool supports ("Left drag: orbit · Scroll: zoom · Arrow keys: turn", "Drag: turn · Pinch: zoom"); the stylesheet shows the one that fits the screen. (3) `.ws-side`: when the form outgrows a screen, a `nav.ws-rail` across its top with one `button.ws-rail__btn` per step, each `<span class="ws-rail__num">2</span>Window` (`aria-current="step"` on the active one; a done one `data-done="yes"` with `<span class="ws-sr">, done</span>`), then `.ws-controls` with the step title, its fieldsets, and Back and Next in a `.ws-nav`; a short form is `.ws-controls` alone (D31). (4) Planned, not yet a component: an edge toggle on the sidebar's border that collapses it so the stage gets the full width.

The tool's 3D view is a `canvas` placed directly in `.ws-stage__view`: the stylesheet makes it fill the view and never size it, so a resize cannot feed back into itself, and turns off the browser's own touch gestures on it (`touch-action:none`) so drag and pinch reach the view. **Without a pointer (D30).** Every move has a press and a key, and the view has a name. Give the canvas `tabindex="0"`, `role="img"`, an `aria-label` naming the part ("The wall in 3D") and `aria-describedby` pointing at the plan line's `id`, so a screen reader hears the name and then the plan. Focused, it shows a ring inside the view, and the tool's script turns the camera with the arrow keys (Left and Right round the part, Up and Down over it) and zooms with `+` and `-`, the same steps as the buttons. "Zoom in" and "Zoom out" (`&#xeb81;`, `&#xeb82;`) step the zoom and stop where a pinch stops. The views are a `Choice` (`role="radiogroup"`, `aria-label="View"`): picking one turns the camera to face that side, framed as above; the side the camera faces is chosen, and a drag or a key that turns it away clears the choice, so this is the one Choice without a standing default; "Reset view" goes back to the first view. **On a phone (D36).** Below the breakpoint, and on a short screen, the view shows only "Reset view" and "View…", so it stays clear for the fingers that pinch and drag; View… opens the rest in a menu below the view, over the plan bar, so the part stays in sight while zooming (D42), one press away for anyone who cannot. The markup is the same on every screen: after Reset view, `<button class="ws-btn ws-stage__more" type="button" popovertarget="ws-view-menu">View…</button>`, then `<div class="ws-stage__menu" id="ws-view-menu" popover>` holding Zoom in, Zoom out and the `.ws-choice.ws-stage__views` choice. On a wide screen the stylesheet hides View… and lays the menu's contents out in place: the zoom buttons beside Reset view, the choice at the bottom left. The browser opens the menu, and closes it on Escape or a press outside it; the tool's script closes it when a view is picked, so the new view shows at once (D42), with the lines below. Where a browser cannot anchor the menu to the view, it opens in the middle of the screen, over the part. The controls wrap rather than overlap, and a drag between them still reaches the canvas. An `svg` drawing standing in for the canvas (as in the preview) is laid out the same way.

Closing View… when a view is picked, in the tool's script (`check-previews.py` runs these lines on the preview, picks a view, and fails if the menu stays open):

```js
for (const menu of document.querySelectorAll('.ws-stage__menu'))
  menu.addEventListener('change', (e) => {
    if (e.target.closest('.ws-stage__views') && menu.matches(':popover-open')) menu.hidePopover();
  });
```

A `p.ws-stage__message` (`aria-live="polite"`) in the view says what is happening while the part loads or when it cannot be drawn, on a `surface-raised` backing so it reads over the model; empty, it takes no room but stays in the page, so it is announced when it fills.

The part in the stage always fits it (DESIGN.md): framed to `--stage-fit` when the part or its size changes, pulled back only as far as needed when the stage resizes (keeping the user's angle), zoom stopping at `--stage-min-fill`, no panning off the stage, and "Reset view" back to the first view.

The stage is always mounted and always visible. On a phone the stage lays its parts out in the page (`display:contents`): the view, `--stage-phone-h` tall, and the plan bar are pinned, the status bar scrolls away with the form, and the page's `scroll-padding` keeps a focused field below the pinned part; under `--breakpoint-short` nothing is pinned. On a screen `--breakpoint-wide` or wider, or at least `--landscape-ratio` wide and `--breakpoint-side-min` (48rem) or wider, the stage takes the full height beside the sidebar (`panel-width`, at most half the screen) (D25). A landscape screen narrower than that stacks, as a phone held upright does, and pins nothing, so the view keeps `--stage-phone-h` and the page scrolls: beside the sidebar its view was a strip, 111px on a 740 × 360 phone (D41). A short screen (`--breakpoint-short` or less tall) goes side by side only from `--breakpoint-side-short` (61rem): narrower, the plan bar wrapped to two or three rows in the stage's column and the view fell under 40 %, 111px on an 800 × 360 phone, so it stacks and pins nothing too, large phones held sideways (844 × 390, 932 × 430) among them (D52). Never put the result under the inputs. On first visit show a short welcome dialog (a quick start in 4 numbered steps and a line that everything runs in the browser); keep it dismissible and show it once.
